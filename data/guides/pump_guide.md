# Pump guide: 3–6 V mini submersible pump

Written by the FixVision author for the demo rig. Not taken from any manufacturer manual. Each section has an id that guidance must cite.

## G-SAFETY: Safety and stop conditions (every job)

- Work only on low-voltage DC pumps (battery or USB). Never connect the pump or its wires to mains power.
- Switch the pump off before putting your hands in the water or touching the intake.
- Run the pump only while it is fully under water. A pump running in air heats up and wears quickly.
- Keep the battery, switch and bare wire joints dry and away from the water container.
- Keep each test run short (about a minute) when the supply is above the pump's rated voltage, such as a 9 V battery on a 3–6 V pump.

**Stop and escalate to a senior technician if:**
- the pump body is cracked, melted or smells burnt;
- the cable insulation is damaged or a wire joint is exposed near the water;
- the pump does not run at all with a known-good supply and a clear intake;
- the pump gets hot to the touch after a short run;
- the fault is not a blocked intake (out of scope for this guide).

## G-F1: Blocked inlet

**Symptoms**
- The pump hums and water comes out, but the receiving container fills much slower than with a healthy pump.
- The stream from the hose is thin or pulses.
- In a bad case almost no water comes out although the pump is clearly running.

**Likely causes**
- Debris, tissue, cloth, hair or tape covering the intake hole or slots.
- A build-up of grit or algae inside the intake.

**Diagnostic check**
1. Switch the pump off.
2. Lift the pump out of the water and look at the intake opening (the hole or slots on the pump body that water is drawn in through).
3. Hold the intake close to the camera so it can be seen clearly. A blocked intake shows material over or inside the opening.

**Repair steps**
1. Switch the pump off and take it out of the water. *(requires approval)*
2. Remove anything covering or stuck in the intake by hand. Do not push a sharp tool into the pump body. *(requires approval)*
3. Rinse the intake in clean water until the opening is clear.
4. Record a close-up of the clean intake with this job's code in view.
5. Put the pump back fully under water at the same spot.
6. Run the functional test: code in view, switch on, fill the jar to the line, switch off.

**Stop conditions for this fault**
- The blockage cannot be removed without opening the pump body: stop and escalate.
- After cleaning, the fill is still much slower than healthy: stop; the cause may be wear, a kinked hose or a weak supply, which this guide does not cover.
